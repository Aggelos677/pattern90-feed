One table, three lenses. The row never changes — star, kick-off, match, three cells, signals, money. Only what sits inside the three cells changes: our fair price against the market, the price path since the market opened, or the two books side by side. Open any row and you get all of it at once, whichever lens you arrived from.
Why it matters. None of the three means much alone. A price that fell, money arriving behind it, and our own model agreeing — that is a signal. Any one of them on its own is an observation.
