How to read a move. Move = opening odds ÷ current odds − 1. A positive move means the price shortened; a negative move means it drifted. The headline selection of each match is the one whose probability changed most — odds percentages alone overstate moves on long shots. The dots sit on a 0–100% scale of margin-free probability, so distances between rows are comparable. When the goal line changes, the over/under prices are on different lines and are shown faded — read the line change instead.
What it is not. Market movement is context, not a tip. Pattern90 fair odds are computed without market prices; a move away from our fair means the gap grew and the model should be re-checked before any tip.
